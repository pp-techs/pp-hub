# M42 — Personal spaces and external sharing

✅ done · part of the [delivery milestones index](../milestones.md). The design
in force is [personal-spaces.md](../personal-spaces.md).

- **The decision.** One account, many spaces: a personal space ("My Drive") is
  a local space (`spaces.kind = 'personal'`) with no Logto organization, and
  sharing crosses spaces through an **external grant** — a direct share flagged
  `is_external` — rather than guest membership, because a surface that has not
  learned about it refuses the outsider instead of exposing anything.
- **Switches.** `system_config` `personal_spaces` (`off` | `members` |
  `everyone`, default `off`), `default_personal_quota_bytes` (required above 0
  for `everyone`), `external_sharing_enabled` and `mcp_external_shares` (both
  off), in Admin › Settings › Personal use; per space `spaces.external_sharing`
  (off on an organization space, on on a personal one) in Space settings ›
  General.
- **Provisioning** at sign-in through a new `auth.SignInHook`
  (`internal/personal`), and again on `GET /spaces` and `GET /users/me` for
  older sessions; `spaces.EnsurePersonal` is idempotent under the
  `spaces_personal_owner_uniq` index. A deleted Logto account archives its
  personal space; `GET|DELETE /admin/personal-spaces` lists and purges them.
- **Resolver.** `NodeAccessCaps` honours, for a non-member, only live external
  direct shares, and only while the instance and the space allow it and the
  space is not archived — never group grants, never ownership.
- **Sharing by email.** `POST /nodes/{id}/shares` with `email`: member →
  ordinary grant; account outside → external grant; no account →
  `pending_shares` row, 202 and an invitation mail, adopted at the first
  verified sign-in. 50 new outsiders per sender per day (429). Audit kinds
  `pending_share_created|adopted|revoked`; `share_created` carries `external`.
- **Outsider routes.** `GET /shared?scope=external`,
  `GET /nodes/{id}/children|path|people`, `GET|DELETE
/nodes/{id}/pending-shares[/{id}]`; creating inside a shared folder is gated by
  `requireParentWrite` alone. Search, Starred and Recent include spaces where
  the caller holds an external grant. Web: `/drive/shared`, `/open/{nodeId}`,
  "My Drive" labelling, email entry and pending rows in the share dialog.
- **Personal spaces refuse administration** they do not have (409): members,
  groups, departures, team folders, linking, deletion, and owner changes to
  slug, default role, quota or external sharing.
- **MCP.** `grant_access` takes `email`; an external grant through an agent
  needs `mcp_external_shares`.
- **Migration** `0019_personal_spaces.sql`.
- **Tests.** `spaces/external_db_test.go` (resolver invariants, provisioning,
  adoption), `httpapi/external_share_db_test.go` (sharing by email, budget,
  outsider routes, personal-space refusals).
- **Hardening after review** (migration `0020_invite_hardening.sql`): sharing
  by email matches verified addresses only (`users.email_verified`); the budget
  counts an append-only `invitation_log`, and one address gets at most 3
  invitation mails a day; invitation mails carry no sender text in the subject
  and no sender links in the body; re-inviting takes covering the invitation;
  adoption honours an invitation only if its inviter still could; an external
  grant stops with its maker's membership, and joining clears the flag;
  `people`, search, Starred and Recent name nothing above an outsider's share,
  and search no longer considers a foreign space beyond the granted subtrees;
  purging does not strand an outsider's content; a personal space is shown to
  outsiders by its owner, not its editable name.
- **Not in it:** billing, cross-space move/copy, external grants to groups,
  re-sharing by outsiders, an instance admin changing a live personal space's
  quota.
