# M29 — Comments and notifications

✅ done · part of the [delivery milestones index](../milestones.md).

- What asked for it: P1, "No comments" in
  [production-readiness.md](../production-readiness.md) — deferred in those
  words since M10 — and the in-app half of notifications that M31's share
  email left open. A comment nobody is told about is a comment nobody answers,
  so the two shipped together.
- **Decision: comments belong to block documents.** A thread is anchored in
  the markdown by a Go-only comment marker pair carrying its id
  (`internal/mdoc`, format in the package documentation and
  [block-documents.md](../block-documents.md#comment-anchors)); the thread and
  its comments are rows. The anchor survives room reloads, version restores
  and downloads because it is in the file. The routes refuse anything but a
  markdown document: an office file or a preview has no place to carry the
  anchor.
- **Decision: taking part takes `CapDownload`, not `CapEdit`.** Whoever can
  open the document can discuss it. Editing a comment is its author's alone;
  deleting is the author's or an editor's; resolving or reopening is the
  thread opener's or an editor's. A reply reopens a resolved thread.
- **Decision: in-app only.** No comment or mention email was built; the M31
  mail path still carries share notifications only.
- Schema: `0015_comments_notifications.sql` — `comment_threads`, `comments`,
  `notifications` ([data-model.md](../data-model.md#comments-and-notifications)).
  Threads cascade with the document's purge (a trashed document keeps them);
  read notifications are purged after 30 days (`notifications.ReadRetention`)
  by the server's five-minute sweep.
- **Who is told.** A new thread notifies the document's owner (`comment`); a
  reply notifies the thread's participants and the owner (`reply`); anyone
  named in the comment's `mentions` gets `mention` instead — once, never the
  actor, and never someone who cannot currently open the document. The rows
  are written in the comment's transaction. A mention written **into the
  document** (`user:<uuid>`) is found by the `notify:mentions` job on the
  version hook, which diffs `mdoc.Mentions` against the previous version and
  records the version so a retry cannot notify twice.
- **Delivery.** `notifications.Broker` fans each committed row out to its
  recipient's open Server-Sent Events streams — at most 16 per user, a stream
  more than 32 events behind is dropped (the client refetches on reconnect).
  The stream writes a heartbeat every 25 s and re-checks its session every
  60 s. The broker is the process's, so the mention job runs in `cmd/server`,
  never in `cmd/worker`, and one server instance serves every stream.
- Routes: `GET /nodes/{id}/comments`, `POST /nodes/{id}/comment-threads`,
  `POST /comment-threads/{id}/comments`, `PATCH`/`DELETE /comments/{id}`,
  `POST /comment-threads/{id}/resolve|reopen`; `GET /notifications`
  (cursor-paged), `GET /notifications/unread-count`,
  `POST /notifications/read {ids? | all}`, `GET /notifications/stream`.
- Web: `lib/comments.ts` (thread store over REST, polled every 10 s while the
  document is open, refetched on focus and after a mutation),
  `components/comments/` (panel, composer with @mention of space members),
  `components/notifications-bell.tsx` in the site and editor headers (unread
  badge, list, mark read, click → `/edit/<id>?thread=<id>`), and
  `lib/event-stream.ts`, an SSE subscription that reconnects with capped
  exponential backoff — `EventSource`'s own retry gives up on the 502 a
  restart behind nginx answers.
- Deployment: nginx `location = /api/v1/notifications/stream` with buffering
  off and a 1 h timeout ([deployment.md](../../deployment.md)).
- Tests: `internal/httpapi/comments_db_test.go` — permission matrix, resolve
  rules, cascade with the document, recipients, unread and mark-read, stream
  delivery to the recipient only, document mentions notifying readers;
  `notifications/broker_test.go`; web `event-stream.test.ts`,
  `comment-mentions.test.ts`. Browser smoke on a cloned database: a comment
  with an @mention raised the other user's badge live (Vietnamese UI), the
  deep link opened the thread, and a mention typed into the document notified
  after write-back.
