# M4 — Office

✅ done · part of the [delivery milestones index](../milestones.md).

- `file_locks` plus the `wopi` module: HMAC-signed tokens, LOCK/UNLOCK/REFRESH
  with conflict semantics, and `GET /wopi/files/{id}` (CheckFileInfo),
  `.../contents` (GetFile/PutFile), `POST .../` (the X-WOPI-Override verbs).
- OnlyOffice 9.x in compose (`:9980`), editor at `/hosting/wopi/{type}/edit`,
  reached by a hidden form POST of `access_token` (9.x dropped the old
  `index.html?wopiSrc=…` GET shim). `PP_WOPI_SERVER_URL` must be
  container-reachable — `host.docker.internal` on macOS.
- Web: `/edit/{nodeId}` opens a full page, not a modal.
- Since then: every conversion request is JWT-signed (M20–M21), the token's rank
  is re-resolved against live grants (M21), and theme + locale follow the app
  (M32).
