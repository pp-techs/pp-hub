# M36 — Sharing hardening

✅ done · part of the [delivery milestones index](../milestones.md).

- `0009_direct_share_uniq.sql`. An audit of the share flow against M15's model
  found ways around it; each is now refused server-side and pinned by database
  tests (`internal/httpapi/sharing_db_test.go`,
  `internal/spaces/permissions_db_test.go`). Details in
  [permissions.md](../permissions.md).
- **Moves are bounded** (`authorizeMove`): ownership inherits, so an editor
  without re-share could otherwise drop the owner's file into their own folder
  and come out owning it. Non-owners may not move into more access than they hold
  nor, without `CapShare`, change who reaches the node, and moving personal
  content into a team folder takes its creator with no foreign content beneath.
- **Changing or revoking a grant takes covering it**, like creating one; removing
  the last manager of a team folder answers 409.
- **One direct grant per (node, person)**: re-sharing upserts,
  `POST /nodes/{id}/shares` takes `user_id` rather than `email` (**breaking** for
  API callers), and "unknown" and "not a member" answer identically.
- Links follow their creator's **download** as well as their share, the
  anonymous landing page no longer falls back to the sharer's email, "Shared with
  me" requires current membership on its granted branch too, and admins cannot
  add themselves to a group that opens files. Every group change is a security
  event.
- **Multi-row writes are transactions** (`store.Queries.InTx`, savepoints when
  nested): move, team-folder creation with its manager grant, purge, space
  creation, add/revoke membership, completing a departure.
- The share dialog shows the whole answer: `GET /nodes/{id}/inherited-access`
  lists owners and ancestor grants, existing grants are edited in place, and
  controls the caller does not hold are left out. `httpapi/permissions_test.go`
  was deleted — it asserted `t.Log` calls, not the product.
