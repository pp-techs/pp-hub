# M38 — Two-way Logto sync

✅ done · part of the [delivery milestones index](../milestones.md).

M33 made a Logto organization the source of truth for membership, but only
membership and the admin role travelled, and some of what the app offered
either did nothing or produced a space nothing reconciles. This milestone
closes both directions. The principle is unchanged: Logto owns membership,
admin role, **name**, and — since Logto became the only provider — **accounts**:
whether a person may use pp-hub, their address and their name. Every app-side
change goes upstream first; every upstream signal means only "organization X"
or "user X changed" and is converged, never applied as a delta — which is also
why a write-through cannot loop.

## Delivered

- **Invitations are accepted at sign-in.** Logto sends an organization
  invitation and leaves acceptance to the application; nothing did, so an
  invitee signed up into an empty drive. `orgsync.AcceptInvitations` accepts
  every live `Pending` invitation for the **verified** email claim with the
  person's Logto user id, and the result is merged into the ID token's claims
  before `ReconcileUser` (the token predates the acceptance). The invitation
  email's `{{link}}` is `{PP_EXTERNAL_URL}/auth/login?login_hint=<address>`;
  `HandleLogin` forwards an address-shaped `login_hint`. Covered by
  `TestSignInAcceptsPendingInvitationsIntoTheSpace`. See
  [logto-org-sync.md](../logto-org-sync.md#accepting-an-invitation).
- **Accounts are Logto's.** A suspension or deletion upstream used to reach
  pp-hub never: Logto refused the next sign-in while a session row kept every
  open tab working for up to 90 days. `orgsync.ReconcileAccount` converges a
  local account against `GET /api/users/{id}` — email and name, IdP profile
  fields, `isSuspended` (sets `suspended_at`, ends every session) — fed by the
  `User.Data.Updated` / `User.SuspensionStatus.Updated` / `User.Deleted`
  webhook (`user:reconcile` jobs), the hourly sweep, and every organization
  reconcile. A deleted account is suspended, not removed, and its
  organizations are reconciled at once. The admin console's Suspend/Reinstate
  writes `PATCH /api/users/{id}/is-suspended` first. `RecordSystem` now keeps
  the account's `user_id`. Covered by the five tests in
  `orgsync/accounts_db_test.go`. See
  [logto-org-sync.md](../logto-org-sync.md#accounts).
- **Logto is the only identity provider.** `config.LogtoSettings.Validate()`,
  called by `cmd/server` (not the worker), refuses startup unless
  `PP_LOGTO_ENDPOINT` and both M2M credentials are set; a missing
  `PP_LOGTO_WEBHOOK_KEY` only warns. The fallbacks for another OIDC provider
  or no Logto tenant are gone — the claims-only mode, `Configured()` /
  `Enabled()`, local space creation without an organization, the conditional
  organization scopes, `orgsync.ErrDisabled` and the 503s that only existed to
  describe those modes. The sign-in always requests
  `urn:logto:scope:organizations` and `urn:logto:scope:organization_roles`.
  An **empty** `organizations` claim now reconciles as "belongs to no
  organization" (departures via the ordinary path); an **absent** one — the
  sign-in application lacks the scopes — is skipped with a warning
  (`auth.reconcileOrgs`) rather than evicting everybody. Covered by
  `TestOrganizationClaimAbsentIsNotEmpty` and
  `TestLogtoRefusesAnIncompleteTenant`. See
  [logto-org-sync.md](../logto-org-sync.md#configuration).
- **Creating a space writes through** (instance admin only, `can_create_spaces`
  = `users.is_admin`). `orgsync.CreateSpace` creates the organization
  (`POST /api/organizations`), adds the creator with the admin role, then
  `ensureSpace` and the creator's membership — so a local failure heals at the
  creator's next sign-in, with nothing to compensate. A creator this Logto
  tenant did not issue gets **422** (`ErrNoIdentity`). No space is created
  unlinked any more.
- **Name syncs both ways.** A rename (`handleUpdateSpace`) PATCHes
  `/api/organizations/{id}` first when the space is linked (`orgsync.Rename`);
  `ReconcileOrg` now GETs the organization before its members and mirrors its
  name into `spaces.name` (`SetSpaceName`). The webhook handles every
  `Organization.*` event, taking the id from `organizationId`, `data.id` or
  `params.id`, and `User.*` from `data.id` or `params.userId`.
- **Deletion does not propagate.** An organization answering 404 to
  `ReconcileOrg` unlinks its space (`unlink`: `idp_org_id = NULL`), keeping
  members and content. At sign-in, before a claims-driven departure, `orgGone`
  looks the organization up: 404 unlinks instead of departing, and a failed
  lookup skips the departure and leaves it to the sweep. Deleting a linked
  space inserts a `detached_orgs(org_id, detached_by)` tombstone in the same
  transaction (migration `0012_detached_orgs.sql`); `ensureSpace` answers "no
  space" for a detached organization, so sign-in never recreates it. The
  organization stays in Logto, and the delete-space dialog says so.
- **"Link to Logto"** for spaces that mirror nothing — legacy ones, or ones
  whose organization was deleted (instance admin).
  `GET /spaces/{id}/link-preview` answers `{pushable[], stranded[]}` (members
  whose `oidc_iss` is this tenant's, and the rest);
  `POST /spaces/{id}/link {confirm_stranded}` answers **409** for unconfirmed
  stranded members
  (`ErrStrandedMembers`) or an already linked space (`ErrAlreadyLinked`),
  otherwise creates the organization synchronously, enqueues an `org:link`
  job carrying `{org_id, space_id}` and answers **202**
  `{status:"linking", idp_org_id}`. `runLink` pushes the pushable members
  (tolerating 422, admin role from the `Admin` group), then `ReattachOrg` and
  `SetSpaceOrg` **last** — so a webhook mid-push cannot evict the members not
  yet pushed — then departs the stranded (source `admin`) and queues a
  reconcile; the organization id in the payload makes a retry idempotent. In
  the web client, `UnlinkedSpaceAlert` on the People pane gives an instance
  admin the button that opens `LinkSpaceModal` (preview, the stranded list,
  and an "I understand they will lose access" checkbox), Invite is disabled
  while the space is unlinked, and `useLinkSpace` polls the space list every
  2s for up to 60s until `idp_org_id` appears. This supersedes M33's manual
  SQL link and its refused backfill. See
  [logto-org-sync.md](../logto-org-sync.md#linking-a-space-to-an-organization).
- **Errors say where the fault is.** `identityProviderError` maps
  `ErrNoIdentity` to 422, `ErrUnreachable` to 503 and an upstream refusal to
  502; `spaceError` maps `ErrNotLinked` to 422 ("an instance administrator can
  link it") and `ErrAlreadyLinked` / `ErrStrandedMembers` to 409.
- The orgsync worker drains `org:reconcile`, `user:reconcile` and `org:link`.

## Verification

- `orgsync/spacesync_db_test.go` — six DB-backed tests: a deleted
  organization unlinks its space and keeps its members; sign-in does not evict
  from a space whose organization was deleted; sign-in does not recreate a
  detached organization's space; reconcile mirrors the organization's name;
  `CreateSpace` seats the creator as admin; `Link` pushes members and departs
  the ones Logto cannot hold.
- `orgsync/accounts_db_test.go` — five DB-backed tests for accounts.
- `auth`: `TestOrganizationClaimAbsentIsNotEmpty`; `config`:
  `TestLogtoRefusesAnIncompleteTenant`.
- The web client was checked in a browser against a mocked API: the unlinked
  alert, the modal's gating on the stranded-members checkbox, and the
  `POST …/link` body carrying `confirm_stranded: true`.
