Final design

Megaminx Hub

A twelve-faced megaminx: every face a space, every piece a file, the solid centre is the hub.

  • Drive, storage, ERP modules and hub in one geometric object
  • Exact 5-fold symmetry: stable and calm
  • Dedicated small-size cuts keep it legible at 16 px
  • Avoids cloud, folder and padlock clichés
